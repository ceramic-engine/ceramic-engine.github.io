PixelArtCrt

View source
ceramic.Entity → ceramic.Shader → shade.Shader__shaders_PixelArtCrt_Vert__shaders_PixelArtCrt_Frag → shaders.PixelArtCrt (Class)

CRT and LCD display simulation for pixel art. Runs once per screen pixel over a low resolution render texture (see ceramic.PixelArt in CRT mode, which owns the parameters and their documentation).

Techniques borrowed, with their origin:

  • crt-lottes by Timothy Lottes (public domain): Gaussian pixel reconstruction (gaus, horz3, horz5), scanline weights (scan and the three-row sum in main), the four phosphor masks (maskTv, maskAperture, maskVgaStretched, maskVga), the warp curvature and the halo + mask composition in linear light.
  • crtemu by Mattias Gustavsson (MIT or public domain): low resolution pre-blur for the halo (rendered by ceramic.PixelArt, sampled here as bloomTex), the vignette formula, grain and flicker.
  • gba-color by Pokefan531, after hunterk's Color Mangler (public domain): color matrix and luminance applied in linear light; the GBA values live in ceramic.PixelArtPreset.
  • SameBoy LCD shader by LIJI32 (MIT): the idea of splitting a pixel in three RGB subpixels with darker gaps (maskLcd), rewritten.
  • crt-geom by cgwg, Themaister and DOLLS (GPL, no code taken): the idea that the beam widens with brightness (beamScale).
  • Pixel art antialiasing as described by Inigo Quilez, Themaister, Claudio Fernandes and Ben Golus: bilinear interpolation limited to a band one output pixel wide around texel edges (sharpTexel).
